About

Building digital tools that businesses actually rely on.

I'm Messabih Warda, a Master's student in Information Technology and Engineering and a freelance digital professional based in Algeria. I design and build modern websites, desktop applications, and promotional videos that help businesses, schools, and entrepreneurs grow their presence — online and off.

My work spans full-stack web development with Laravel and modern front-end tooling, custom desktop software built with C# and .NET for inventory and management needs, and short-form video production for brands that want to show up on social media with intent.

I'm early in my freelance career and I treat that as an advantage: every project gets my full attention, direct communication, and code I'd be proud to hand off. I'm currently taking on a small number of projects so I can give each one the depth it deserves.
